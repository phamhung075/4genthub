## The task-event INSERT states the type of its deduplicated parameter, so the gapless-seq case passes with a database

### Fixed
- **`fastmcp/task_management/infrastructure/repositories/task_event_repository.go`** — the append's `INSERT ... SELECT` now writes `$1::varchar(64)` instead of a bare `$1`. The parameter is used TWICE in one statement, inserted into `user_id` and compared against it, and PostgreSQL deduces a parameter's type once for all of its uses: left alone, the comparison resolves `$1` as `text` (the string category's preferred type, preferred over the column's `varchar`) while the insert slot resolves the column's `VARCHAR(64)` (`task_event_tables.go:33`), and the two resolutions collide as `SQLSTATE 42P08` — `inconsistent types deduced for parameter $1`. The chosen form is the cast on the INSERT SLOT, naming the column's own type, because that states the domain the value must satisfy and leaves the comparison in the column's own type rather than casting the column to `text` in the `WHERE`. The reviewer's attribution verified that one cast suffices on either side, or on both; this is the one, and the second parameter needs nothing, which is why the error named the first alone.
